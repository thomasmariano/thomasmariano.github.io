About Me

I’m a systems engineer who writes software, works with hardware, and likes understanding why something isn’t working.

How I got here

I studied mechanical engineering at the University of Maryland. Working on aerospace ground systems led me further into software: building scheduling tools, processing orbital data, and connecting applications to the systems they support.

I enjoy being able to follow a problem from the hardware into the code. My projects reflect that mix, from a physical stair-lift prototype to Python desktop applications and satellite visualization.

My Background

I graduated from the University of Maryland, College Park with a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering in 2024.

I am currently pursuing a master’s in Computer Science at Georgia Institute of Technology (Georgia Tech), building on my mechanical engineering background and software development experience.

My engineering experience has taken me across several disciplines, including aerospace systems, mechanical design, software development, electronics, testing, manufacturing, and technical analysis.

I currently work as a Systems Engineer supporting NASA's Search and Rescue program at Goddard Space Flight Center through Peraton, where I work with complex aerospace systems spanning hardware, software, operations, and maintenance.

Engineering Approach

The MEOSAR scheduler starts with operational constraints: which satellites are visible, which antennas are available, and which frequency bands each antenna supports. I translated those constraints into scheduling logic and a desktop interface where the resulting assignments can be reviewed and edited.

For the simulator, I connected a Python orbital-data backend to a JavaScript dashboard through JSON. Keeping timestamps, satellite identities, and visibility calculations consistent between the two applications was an important part of the integration. I also separated configuration, interface, and data-handling responsibilities into smaller modules as the applications grew.
