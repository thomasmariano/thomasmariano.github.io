About Me

Systems Engineer with a multidisciplinary background spanning aerospace, software, hardware, and mechanical engineering.

Engineering Across Disciplines

I am an engineer with a background in mechanical engineering and experience spanning aerospace systems, software development, hardware integration, and technical problem solving.

I enjoy working at the intersection of different engineering disciplines and developing practical solutions to complex problems.

My Background

I graduated from the University of Maryland, College Park with a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering in 2024.

My engineering experience has taken me across several disciplines, including aerospace systems, mechanical design, software development, electronics, testing, manufacturing, and technical analysis.

I currently work as a Systems Engineer supporting NASA's Search and Rescue program at Goddard Space Flight Center through Peraton, where I work with complex aerospace systems spanning hardware, software, operations, and maintenance.

Engineering Approach

I enjoy working on problems that cross traditional engineering boundaries. Whether the solution involves software, electronics, mechanical hardware, or a combination of the three, I like understanding how the pieces interact and building practical solutions that work in the real world.

My approach is centered on understanding how a system works, identifying the problem, and developing solutions that are practical, reliable, and maintainable.
